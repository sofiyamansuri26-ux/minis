    Talbina Recipe

    About Talbina

    Talbina is a traditional dish made with barley flour and milk or water. It is mentioned in the teachings of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him)It is a simple and nourishing food that is often consumed for its helth benefits. Talbina is known for its soothing properties and is often recommended for those who are feeling unwell or recovering from illness. It is aiso a comferting dish that can be enjoyed by people of all ages.

    Ingredients

    • 2 tablespoons of barley flour
    • 1 cup of milk or water
    • 1 teaspoon of honey (optional)
    • Dates or nuts (optional)

    Instructions

    1. Pour milk or water into a pan.
    2. Add barley flour slowly and stir well.
    3. Cook on low heat for 5-10 minutes.
    4. Add honey if desired.
    5. Serve warm and enjoy your Talbina.

    Benefits of Talbina

    Barley provides dietary fibre and other nutrients. Talbina is also known for its ability to soothe the stomach and provide relief from digestive issues.
