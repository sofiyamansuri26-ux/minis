    About Maggi

    Maggi is a popular and testy snack.
    It is easy and quick to make.

    Ingredients
    • 1 packet of Magg
    • 1 cup of water
    • Maggi testemaker
    • Chopped vegetables (optional)

    Preparation Time

    2 minutes

    cooking Time

    5 minutes

    Cooking Instructions

    1. Boil water in pan.
    2. Add maggi noodles.
    3. Add the testemaker
    4. cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring occastionally.
    5. Your delicious Maggi is ready to eat!
